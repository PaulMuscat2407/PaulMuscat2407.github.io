description:  Inspired by games like Call of the Wild, I developed a hunting game to explore and recreate some of its core mechanics in a simplified form. The game features a weapon ballistics system that simulates realistic weapon behavior, where different ammunition types vary in travel distance, damage, and drop-off over time. Animals in the game follow dynamic behavioral patterns, such as routines for eating, sleeping, and drinking, moving between designated hotspots to fulfill these needs. Additionally, animals are equipped with sensory detection abilities, including sight and hearing, allowing them to respond to the player’s actions. Prey instinctively flees when sensing danger, while predators may opt to attack, creating a challenging and immersive hunting experience.
